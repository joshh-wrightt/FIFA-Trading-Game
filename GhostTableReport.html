📰 The world that never was · what trading actually did

Strip every trade out of the ledger — hand everyone back their original four teams and their opening bank — and the league table you get is almost unrecognisable. The same matches, the same goals, the same points; only the owners change. And it turns out the owners are the whole game. In this ghost world a different manager lifts the trophy, the real-life runaway leader is becalmed in mid-table, and the player propping up the actual standings would have been comfortably mid-pack. Trading didn't just nudge this competition — it authored it.
