Trade of the day 8 Jun · 16:38

Paulius builds an empire — then bets £3.75 on Sweden

The runaway leader has turned the sweepstakes into a one-man hedge fund. Paulius now sits on 27.85 EV across eight teams — nearly double second place — with the bank squeezed down to 45p. His signature move is the corner: he holds two Mexicos, two Swedens and two Haitis. The boldest stroke came on Monday afternoon, when he prised a second Sweden out of Rohit for £3.75 cash — the single biggest payment in the whole ledger — just hours after squeezing the first out of Echo (who threw in a pound the other way to be rid of it).

On paper it looks reckless: Sweden are a Pot-3 also-ran at 3.28 EV. They finished bottom of their qualifying group on two points and only reached the finals through the Nations League back door, Viktor Gyökeres firing a hat-trick past Ukraine and an 88th-minute winner past Poland under Graham Potter (UEFA). But with Gyökeres and Alexander Isak — two of the Premier League's sharpest strikers — Paulius is betting the market has badly mispriced Sweden's ceiling. And his two Mexicos? They kick the entire tournament off against South Africa at the Estadio Azteca on Thursday (Sky Sports).

Market-maker 8 Jun · 08:23 & 16:39

Michael sells the Haaland story and banks the chips

Two trades, one philosophy. In the morning Michael shipped Norway to Rohit (pocketing £1.50) to grab reigning African champions Ivory Coast — then flipped that exact Ivory Coast to Jing eight hours later for Saudi Arabia plus £2.80. He has churned his way down to five lower-pot teams (two Paraguays, two Uzbekistans, Saudi Arabia) but built one of the fattest banks at the table: £7.65.

The team he let walk could haunt him. Norway are back at a World Cup for the first time since 1998, with Erling Haaland having battered in 16 qualifying goals — including a 4-1 demolition of Italy — alongside captain Martin Ødegaard (Olympics.com). Rohit happily took him on: having also cashed his Sweden to Paulius, Rohit is now a lean two-team play — Germany and Norway — sitting on £6.78. Quality over quantity.

Two-for-one 8 Jun · 11:44

Two managers, opposite instincts: AJ concentrating, Haydee diversifying.
